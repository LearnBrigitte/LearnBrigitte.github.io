- Keep as much distance from D.Mon as possible. She loses most of her value at range.
- Throw Whip Shot to maintain distance while proccing Inspire for extra sustain.
- Avoid getting caught in the open. If D.Mon closes the distance, she will run you over.
- Take and hold high ground for as long as possible to stay out of her reach.
- Use Rally's stun to interrupt her ultimate.
- Use Rally's stun to cancel her Remech.
